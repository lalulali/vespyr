---
name: grill-me
description: End-to-end Socratic alignment loop — stress-tests requirements, specifications, and architecture decisions before implementation. Use when user wants to be grilled on a plan, design, or idea.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this end-to-end plan/design until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree (product requirements, architecture trade-offs, edge cases, and codebase logic), resolving dependencies one-by-one.

Rules:
1. Ask the questions one at a time, providing your recommended answer for each.
2. If a question can be answered by exploring the project documents or codebase, explore them first instead of asking.
3. Once the interview is complete, save all resolved decisions to `artifacts/memory/active-decisions.md`.
